MotoristaOPS Presença follows an executive mobility control-room aesthetic: warm premium neutrals, precise operational typography, and restrained amber journey signals. Light-first with a complete dark mode. Pacing is tight for dashboard and workflow surfaces, with 8px-based rhythm, compact cards, tabular numerals, hairline borders, minimal shadows, and sentence case. Instrument Sans carries all interface text; Bebas Neue is reserved for selective display moments and status numerals. Marfim is the main surface, Noite/Grafite provide contrast, Pedra supports secondary information, Champagne is the premium accent, and Âmbar is used only for route/progress/attention. Photography and decorative gradients are excluded from operational UI. Radius is modest, never pill-heavy except for compact statuses. Accessibility, direct hierarchy, and predictable state communication take precedence over ornament.
