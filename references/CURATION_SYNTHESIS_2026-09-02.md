# MotoristaOPS V2 - Curadoria consolidada

Status: INCORPORATED INTO WIREFRAME GATE
Reviewed: 2026-09-02
Source package: `motoristaops-v2-references.zip`

## Diagnosis

The package is coherent and operational rather than a loose moodboard. It separates Landing, Instagram, Dashboard, Visual System, Iconography, Content/Prompts, QA/Governance and agent templates. Its strongest value is not any single visual reference but the rules for extracting function, structure, visual criterion, risk and application.

## What enters the Landing now

### Primary architecture
- internal MotoristaOPS full-page / hero: page responsibilities, service tone, method, direct CTA and human proof;
- Elite Auto Concierge: explain a personal service through a short transparent process and reduction of friction;
- MotoristaOPS visual system: premium functional, dark/champagne/off-white direction, strong hierarchy, controlled whitespace, real photography.

### Secondary visual rhythm
- Sahara Velocity: cinematic pacing and large -> medium -> large rhythm;
- Aurex Motors: journey/route narrative and controlled automotive imagery;
- Luca Sterling: editorial organization around a real person without turning the site into a personal portfolio.

### Downgraded / tool-only
- Apex Motors: modular commercial patterns only; dealership/inventory architecture is a mismatch;
- Supahero: discovery library for hero patterns, not authority;
- Refero: section/pattern lookup, not authority.

## What stays out of Landing

- Dashboard references and product UI density;
- shadcn/dashboard conventions;
- invented metrics, testimonials or fleet claims;
- dealership inventory structures;
- supercar/exotic aspiration;
- fake executive-driver imagery;
- luxury theater and black-gold ornament;
- social virality tactics without service relevance.

## Landing grammar derived from the curation

1. Direct value proposition.
2. One dominant conversion: WhatsApp.
3. Method as differentiation.
4. Real person + real car as proof.
5. Short service taxonomy.
6. Trust through observable behavior, not adjectives.
7. Instagram as continuity/proof, not primary conversion.
8. Final CTA repeats the direct-contact logic.
9. Footer closes navigation and contact without adding new propositions.

## Process rule adopted

No high-fidelity section can become a baseline before the complete low-fidelity landing wireframe is structurally approved.

High-fidelity sessions may use at most three primary references each and must declare: what is absorbed, what is rejected, and which MotoristaOPS contract has authority.
