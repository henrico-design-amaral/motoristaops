# MotoristaOPS V2 — Landing Section Contracts v0.1

Status: ACTIVE — CONTENT/PRODUCT CONVERGENCE
Parent: `landing-wireframe-v0.2.md`
Scope: Header + sections 03–11. Hero has its own contract in `landing-hero-v0.1.md`.

## Global rules
- One dominant conversion: WhatsApp.
- No invented metrics, reviews, credentials, years of experience, customer types, operating hours or service areas.
- Copy is direct, factual and short. No generic luxury/executive promises.
- Each section must have one job. Remove blocks that repeat the same proof or proposition.
- Public landing is not dashboard UI and does not expose operational complexity.
- `MotoristaOPS`, `MOTORISTA PARTICULAR`, `por Henrico Amaral` and the approved slogan follow brand authority.

## 01 Header
### Job
Recognition, orientation and immediate contact.
### Required
- official wordmark/approved compact brand asset;
- anchors: Serviços, Como funciona, Quem dirige; optional `Avaliações` only if real review block exists;
- primary CTA: WhatsApp.
### Avoid
- theme/language toggles in initial V2 unless reinstated by explicit product decision;
- dense navigation;
- generic `Sobre nós` when `Quem dirige` is the actual content.
### Mobile
Compact brand + WhatsApp/contact trigger + minimal menu when needed.

## 03 Microbenefits / proposal quick scan
### Job
Explain the value in <10 seconds without repeating the Hero.
### Max
3–4 items.
### Approved axes for working copy
- Pontualidade / compromisso com horário.
- Comunicação clara.
- Cuidado no trajeto.
- Discrição / respeito ao contexto do passageiro.
### Evidence rule
Write as behavior/service principle, not superlative claim.

## 04 Services
### Job
Make occasions of use concrete.
### Current service taxonomy
- Aeroportos e rodoviárias.
- Reuniões e compromissos.
- Eventos.
- Viagens e deslocamentos particulares.
- Espera/acompanhamento when actually contracted.
### Content rule
Use everyday customer language. Do not create `executive`, `corporate`, `VIP`, `luxury` tiers without a real commercial policy.
### Interaction
Cards/rows can deep-link or prefill WhatsApp intent later; initial implementation may keep one global CTA.

## 05 How it works
### Job
Reduce uncertainty between interest and ride.
### Four-step working model
1. Você chama — tells date/time/origin/destination/need.
2. A gente alinha — route, timing, stops/waiting and relevant details.
3. Você confirma — price/conditions and booking are confirmed through the official channel.
4. Eu cuido do trajeto — service is executed with communication and agreed conditions.
### Copy rule
Human language; no SaaS/process jargon.
### Contract dependency
Final wording must match actual booking, cancellation, reservation and waiting policies.

## 06 Experience / differentiators
### Job
Explain why the way Henrico works matters.
### Permitted proof axes
- real work routine as driver;
- +2000 Uber trips and 4.96 rating only if/while current evidence is available for public use;
- knowledge of routes/context from daily operation;
- communication, discretion and care;
- operational discipline around scheduling and waiting.
### Forbidden without evidence
- invented years of experience;
- defensive-driving certifications not documented;
- English/intermediate language claims not explicitly approved;
- `executive profile`, `high-standard fleet`, `VIP`, `premium vehicles` as credentials.
### Principle
Evidence > adjective.

## 07 Real proof of service
### Job
Show the real operation before asking the user to trust marketing copy.
### Source priority
1. real vehicle photos;
2. real interior photos;
3. real work-context photos that do not expose clients/private data;
4. operational artifacts safe for public display.
### Current asset choice
The existing HB20 photos are suitable here. Parking/background can be reframed/cropped, but the vehicle must stay real.
### Privacy
Blur/hide plate, client faces, addresses, documents and private trip information when applicable.
### Avoid
Stock interior, generated luxury car or airport imagery presented as proof.

## 08 Who drives
### Job
Humanize after service/value/proof are already clear.
### Image
Use one approved real portrait: candidate preference `perfil-normal` or `perfil-portfolio`; `perfil-sério` is reserved for contexts where a harder expression is beneficial.
### Content structure
- name: Henrico Amaral;
- one short paragraph: how he approaches the work;
- 2–3 factual trust anchors that are already evidenced elsewhere.
### Avoid
Long biography, career portfolio, invented credentials or suit-and-tie persona inconsistent with actual service.

## 09 Social proof
### Job
External confirmation immediately before final conversion.
### Required
- 2 or 3 real Google reviews only;
- source must be verified at publication time;
- exact or faithfully excerpted wording;
- reviewer first name/abbreviation as permitted by public source;
- star rating if present in source.
### Never
- fabricate names/reviews;
- rewrite sentiment and display it as a quote;
- show Google branding in a misleading way.
### Fallback
If verified reviews cannot be retrieved, remove the section rather than use placeholders in production.

## 10 Final CTA
### Job
Close the page with one clear action.
### Working message
`Precisa combinar um trajeto? Fale comigo pelo WhatsApp.`
### Supporting line
May reinforce scheduling and alignment, but cannot promise immediate availability.
### CTA
`Chamar no WhatsApp`.

## 11 Footer
### Job
Utility, closure and traceability.
### Required
- MotoristaOPS / approved brand lockup;
- contact link(s) that are actually active;
- navigation anchors where useful;
- Instagram only if official/current channel is confirmed;
- legal links only when documents/pages actually exist.
### Avoid
Fake address, fake phone, fake email, generic policy links or `©` year hardcoded unnecessarily.

## Cross-section responsive contract
- No horizontal scroll at 320 CSS px.
- Reading order on mobile follows narrative order, not desktop geometry.
- CTAs stay reachable and clear; repeated CTAs are intentional, not decorative duplication.
- Evidence images preserve meaningful crop.
- Cards may become stacked lists; no tiny multi-column grids on mobile.
- `Quem dirige` stacks image + text without forcing portrait to dominate first viewport.
- Reviews stack or carousel only if keyboard/touch/accessibility behavior is proven; simple stacked cards are preferred initially.

## Convergence gate
This contract can advance to high-fidelity layout section-by-section without human intervention. Human approval is required before:
- selecting a visual Hero baseline;
- freezing a portrait when two materially different expressions remain viable;
- publishing unverified review/customer evidence;
- changing operational/commercial policy;
- releasing/deploying the public page.
