## Why it exists
MotoristaOPS unifies three connected but distinct responsibilities: public acquisition/service presentation, driver operation/measurement and social distribution. It should make the service understandable and contractable, make the professional operation traceable and improvable, and keep public communication consistent.

## Primary surfaces
1. Landing/public service — `www.motoristaops.com.br`.
2. Operational dashboard — `dashboard.motoristaops.com.br`.
3. Social media/content system.

## Audience / jobs
- People and organizations that need a reliable private-driver service.
- Henrico as operator who needs to register, understand and improve the driving business.
- Public/social audiences that need clear service context before direct contact.

## Proposition
A human private-driver service supported by a deliberate operational system. Public communication must remain factual and trust-oriented; operational tooling must support real decisions rather than decorative metrics.

## Scope now
- public landing and direct contact/conversion;
- private-driver service presentation;
- operational/financial dashboard and import workflows;
- social content and publishing contracts;
- brand consistency across surfaces without forcing one interface grammar onto all three.

## Durable non-goals
- Landing is not a dashboard skin.
- Social is not an independent brand.
- Dashboard does not require authentication by default unless a newer decision changes that.
- No unsupported luxury/performance claims or invented evidence.

## Success signals
- public proposition and contact path are clear;
- approved brand/identity survives every surface;
- dashboard data is traceable and useful for decisions;
- social pieces remain coherent with real service;
- another executor can continue from repository contracts without chat history.
