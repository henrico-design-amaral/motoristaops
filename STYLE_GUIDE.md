# MotoristaOPS — Style Guide Resolver

Status: ACTIVE
Version: 1.0
Date: 2026-09-01
Authority: current Brand Book V12 + official assets + `DESIGN.md` + accepted surface decisions

## Purpose
This file makes the current expressive authority portable without duplicating or replacing the Brand Book.

## Brand architecture
- Project name: `MotoristaOPS` — always joined.
- Human name when used: `Henrico Amaral`.
- Signature when applicable: `por Henrico Amaral`.
- Category when required by the approved brand: `MOTORISTA PARTICULAR`.
- Slogan/tagline usage follows current Brand Book/approved surface decisions; it is not part of the logo unless explicitly defined there.

## Expression principles
- direct, human, factual and service-specific;
- minimal without becoming generic or neutral;
- trust through real operation, clarity, care and evidence rather than luxury clichés;
- public/social surfaces may be expressive; dashboard remains operational and scan-oriented;
- no provider-default aesthetic may fill an unresolved brand decision.

## Visual authority
Resolve exact current values/assets from Brand Book V12 and official asset sources before production. `DESIGN.md` defines the authority order and fail-closed behavior.

Applicable areas:
- logo/crest/wordmark/signature;
- typography families and roles;
- palette roles and combinations;
- iconography family and official icons;
- photography/art direction;
- composition personality;
- motion personality;
- channel/surface adaptations.

## Asset lock
When an official asset exists, use the real asset. Never redraw, approximate, regenerate or replace:
- logo/crest/wordmark;
- signature;
- official icons;
- protected brand graphics or approved photography where specified.

## Surface adaptation
### Landing
Memorable, high-impact and restrained; service reality and conversion dominate. Avoid dashboard/SaaS density.

### Dashboard
Operational clarity, reliable hierarchy and decision support. Brand anchors remain, but density and component behavior may differ from the public surface.

### Social
Extends the public identity and campaign system. It does not become an independent visual language.

## Writing relationship
Use HenricoOPS professional writing mode unless a current content protocol overrides it. Copy should be concise, specific and factual; avoid generic chauffeur/luxury promises and unsupported claims.

## Forbidden drift
- `Motorista OPS` or other split naming when naming the product;
- wrong human-name variants;
- ad-hoc icon families/emoji replacing official iconography;
- generative recreation of official identity assets;
- ungoverned colors/fonts;
- generic premium/glow/SaaS aesthetics disconnected from the service;
- transferring dashboard composition to landing/social by convenience.

## Validation
Before visual acceptance, verify against `DESIGN.md`, current Brand Book/asset authority, A11Y and the applicable protocol/gates.
