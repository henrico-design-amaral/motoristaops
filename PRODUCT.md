## Product model
MotoristaOPS is a private-driver service and operating system with three first-class surfaces:

1. **Public Landing** — presents the service, builds trust and converts to direct contact.
2. **Operational Dashboard** — records, analyzes and improves the driver's financial/operational work.
3. **Social Media** — creates discovery, recurrence and relationship around the real service.

These surfaces share project truth and brand anchors but do not share identical interface density or composition.

## Public service
The public surface may present real private-driver use cases such as scheduled rides, airports, bus terminals, meetings, events, commitments and travel when those remain supported by current service decisions.

Primary conversion path: direct contact, principally WhatsApp.

## Operational capabilities
The dashboard supports, according to current project control:
- daily/weekly/monthly/yearly closing;
- platform and private-ride revenue;
- hours and kilometers;
- operating expenses, fuel and vehicle cost;
- targets, efficiency and performance indicators;
- assisted Uber-data import and traceability;
- attachments/history/filtering/search;
- strategic, financial, operational, maintenance, fuel and tax-oriented readings;
- spreadsheet/import integrations where currently governed.

## Critical boundaries
- Dashboard authentication is not mandatory by default.
- Imported video/OCR processing remains local unless explicitly changed.
- Imported data is not persisted without the review behavior defined by current decisions.
- Landing does not inherit dashboard navigation/density.
- Social is not an independent product or identity.
- No invented metrics, testimonials, service guarantees or availability claims.
